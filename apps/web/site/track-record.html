bestmodel.run — track record

Trust is a ladder,
climbed by verified acts.

A track record states which part of the verification chain a contributor has performed. Each level is granted by a verified act — never self-declared, and it does not decay. Every act is attributable to an Ed25519 key.

start contributing →

level 01

Contributor

You run benchmarks on your own hardware and submit signed results. One machine, one voice — but a real one, on the record.

granted by: a validated signed run

level 02

Replicator

You independently reproduce other contributors' runs — confirming or contradicting the reported numbers. A claim is one machine and one voice; a replication is the same numbers surviving different hands.

granted by: validated reproductions

threshold: proposal — dial pending owner

level 03

Auditor

You defend the pool: replicate high-impact cells and report unreal runs through moderation. A confirmed fake credits the reporter — the pool polices itself.

granted by: a confirmed fake caught + reproductions

threshold: proposal — dial pending owner

Scoring (from the contributor export)

validated signed run×2 · live
confirmed fake caught×5 · live
reproduction of another contributor's run×3 · lands with the reproduce flow

Levels assume the one before them: a contributor who never reproduces another party's run stays a contributor. Points derive from signed activity only — nothing is handed out.

Why does replication outrank claiming? Because a claim is one machine and one voice; a replication is the same numbers surviving different hands. The community measures, the CLI signs, anyone can replicate — and every level below is computed from that signed record, nothing else.
