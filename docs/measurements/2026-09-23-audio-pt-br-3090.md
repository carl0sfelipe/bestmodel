## Honesty

- Do not answer Portuguese ASR from the L4 4.82×real claim.
- Do not call the WER `measured` until n≥3 full passes.
- Meeting audio is harder than FLEURS read speech. Expect WER to rise.
- `canirunit` has no `audio_xreal` task yet (see `specs/en/L07-native-audio-bench.md`).
