How scoring works
  1. measured — the median of many community runs on your exact rig and quantization.
  2. reported — one or two community runs on the same rig, shown honestly.
  3. extrapolated — scaled by memory bandwidth from the closest tested rig.
  4. formula — a published VRAM rule when no measurement exists yet.
  5. no data yet — when we have nothing, we say so instead of inventing a number.
