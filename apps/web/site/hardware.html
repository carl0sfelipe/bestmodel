    // honesty ladder

    1. measured — median of many community runs on the exact rig and quantization.
    2. reported — one or two runs on the same rig, shown as reported.
    3. extrapolated — scaled by memory bandwidth from the closest tested rig.
    4. formula — a published VRAM rule when no measurement exists yet.
    5. no data yet — we say so instead of inventing a number.
